How this site handles information.

A short, honest account of what this website does and does not collect.

What this website collects

As published in this proposal, the site collects nothing. There is no analytics provider, no advertising pixel, no cookie banner because no cookies are set, and no form data is transmitted to any server.

The enquiry forms

The appointment and consulting forms do not send data to this website or to any third party. Submitting one opens your own email application with the message prepared, which you then send yourself. Your information travels through your own email provider, not through this site.

You are asked not to include symptoms, medical history or other health information in those forms.

Fonts and embedded content

Typefaces are served from this website itself rather than a third-party font service, so loading a page does not reveal your visit to another company.

The contact page embeds a map from OpenStreetMap. Loading that map involves a request to openstreetmap.org, which is subject to its own privacy policy.

If analytics are added later

The site contains inactive hooks for conversion tracking. They do nothing unless an analytics provider is installed. If the practice adds one, this policy and the site's consent handling must be updated first.

Health information

To be completed by the practice. How patient information is handled in the office, retention periods, patient rights, and any notice obligations that apply are matters for the practice and its advisers. Nothing has been written here on the practice's behalf.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the practice using the details on the contact page.
